Dungeon Party Finder
Requires HLX Core 0.0.8 or newer and Better Mod Settings.
Install with Vortex, or extract the archive into the Farever game folder.
Other players need the mod too: it finds only players who use it.

How to use:
- Click the DPF button on the screen: the list of dungeons opens. Click a dungeon.
- In the window choose a difficulty and press "Search". A circle turns and "Searching..." is
  shown; "Cancel" stops the search. You are matched with players who chose the same dungeon and
  difficulty and are in the same level bracket: 1-3, 4-7, 8-10, 11-14, 15-18, 19-20, 21-24, 25.
- The player who started earliest is the leader. The others get the game's group invitation:
  accept it. Then the button becomes "Start" (leader) or "Ready" (the others), as in the game's
  own dungeon window.
- The group leader wears a small crown in the party list on the left of the screen.

Settings (Esc -> Mod Settings -> Dungeon Party Finder): show the button, "Unpin" (hold the left mouse
button on the DPF button and drag it), button size, players needed to start (4 = full group), log.

The windows and the settings follow the client's language (English or Russian).
Matching uses the platform's lobbies through the game's own multiplayer code (Steam lobbies on
Steam); the mod has no server of its own.
