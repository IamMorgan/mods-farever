Dungeon Party Finder
Requires HLX Core 0.0.8 or newer and Better Mod Settings.
Install with Vortex, or extract the archive into the Farever game folder.
Other players need the mod too: it finds only players who use it.

How to use:
- Click the DPF button on the screen: the list of dungeons opens (ten rows, scroll for more). Click a dungeon.
- The game's own dungeon window opens. Choose a difficulty and press "Search" under "Start". A circle
  turns and "Searching..." is shown; "Cancel" stops the search. You are matched with players who chose
  the same dungeon and difficulty and are in the same level bracket: 1-3, 4-7, 8-10, 11-14, 15-18,
  19-20, 21-24, 25.
- The player who started earliest is the leader. The others get the game's group invitation: accept
  it. If someone declines, the leader invites another searcher in their place. Then the window is
  the group's dungeon lobby: "Start" for the leader, "Ready" for the others.
- The group leader wears a small crown in the party list on the left of the screen.

Settings (Esc -> Mod Settings -> Dungeon Party Finder): show the button, "Unpin" (hold the left mouse
button on the DPF button and drag it), button size.

The windows and the settings follow the client's language (all 14 languages of the game; English for any other).
Matching uses the platform's lobbies through the game's own multiplayer code (Steam lobbies on
Steam); the mod has no server of its own.
