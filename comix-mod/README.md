## Farever Comix

Comic-style effects for Farever. Requires [hlx-core](https://github.com/hlx-framework/hlx-core/releases) and [Better Mod Settings](https://github.com/xWink/farever-mods/tree/main/better-mod-settings).

### Features

- **Speech bubbles:** a chat message appears in a bubble over the sender's head. Visibility follows the chat rules: a private message is shown to its sender and recipient, a public one to everyone, a group one to the group members.
- **Magic damage numbers** are light blue, physical ones stay default.
- **Critical hits:** the number is red and flies to the right of the other numbers inside a comic burst with a random word (CRASH, WHAM, SNAP, SMASH, BOOM, CRACK, BAM, GRRR, POW) above it. Only one burst exists at a time: a new crit replaces the previous one, and a burst stays at most 2 seconds.
- **Resolution:** sizes of all effects scale with the game's resolution.
- **Settings:** checkboxes *Enable the mod*, *Enable speech bubbles*, *Enable damage effects*, and an *Image size* slider (30–200%, default 100%).
